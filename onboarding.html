One more thing about the fn key

macOS gives fn / 🌐 its own job by default — usually the emoji picker. Set it to Do Nothing so it's yours alone: System Settings → Keyboard → "Press 🌐 key to".

  1. ⌨️
    How to dictate

    Press Win + Alt + Space to start, and again to stop — or hold it to talk and release when you're done. Your words are typed straight into whatever field you're in.

  2. 🎙️
    The Flow Bar

    A small bar floats at the bottom of your screen and never steals focus. Click its mic to dictate, right-click it for options, or drag it anywhere.

  3. 🎤
    Microphone

    Voxable uses your microphone locally. Windows allows this for desktop apps by default; if it's ever blocked, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → Let desktop apps access your microphone.
