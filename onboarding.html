Welcome to Voxable

Two permissions and you're set. Voxable transcribes on your machine — audio never leaves it.

  1. 🎤
    Microphone Checking…

    So Voxable can hear you. Required.

  2. ⌨️
    Accessibility Checking…

    Two things need this: the fn key starting dictation from any app, and pasting the result into whatever you're typing in. Without it Voxable still works — you press the button in the Flow Bar and paste yourself.

    After switching Voxable on in System Settings, come back here — this screen notices on its own.

fn
One more thing about the fn key

macOS gives fn / 🌐 its own job by default — usually the emoji picker. Set it to Do Nothing so it's yours alone: System Settings → Keyboard → "Press 🌐 key to".
